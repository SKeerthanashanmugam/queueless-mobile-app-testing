# Exploratory Testing Charters

## Charter 1 — Booking race conditions (60 min)

Explore rapid taps, two users selecting the same slot, stale slot screens, back navigation and retries. Look for duplicate appointments, inconsistent availability and unclear recovery.

## Charter 2 — Payment uncertainty (60 min)

Explore network loss, delayed callbacks, app backgrounding, repeated Pay taps and gateway return paths. Look for duplicate charges, orphan payments and misleading success/failure messages.

## Charter 3 — Accessibility and older users (45 min)

Use large fonts, TalkBack/VoiceOver and one-handed navigation. Look for unlabeled controls, focus traps, clipped information and small touch targets.

## Charter 4 — Privacy (45 min)

Inspect notifications, app switcher preview, logs visible through normal authorized tooling, screenshots and account switching. Use synthetic data only; do not attempt unauthorized access.
